PTZ camera requirement checklist

1. Coverage

Room dimensions, camera-to-subject distance, stage width, seated participants and desired framing.

2. Video path

USB, HDMI, SDI or IP/NDI; existing codec, PC, switcher, display and network constraints.

3. Audio & control

Microphone/DSP integration, presets, touch-panel control, remote operation and recording or streaming use.

4. Installation

Wall, ceiling or tripod position; cable route, power, lighting, commissioning and user handover requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Can a PTZ camera work with Microsoft Teams or Zoom?

Yes, subject to the room system and connection method. Share whether the room uses a dedicated codec, PC or BYOD setup so compatibility can be checked before selection.

What information is needed for a PTZ camera quotation?

Please share room photos/layout, camera-to-subject distance, primary use case, required platform, existing equipment, delivery city and installation requirement.

Do you supply a complete auditorium or smart-classroom camera workflow?

Yes. A project can include cameras along with displays, switching, microphones, DSP, recording, streaming, cabling, installation and commissioning based on the approved scope.
