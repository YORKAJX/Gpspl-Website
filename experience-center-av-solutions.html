Directional parametric speakers create acoustic 'sound spotlights'. A visitor standing directly under an exhibit hears narration in crystal clarity, while someone standing just 2 feet away hears absolute silence. Zero noisy audio clutter.

  • Concealed architectural in-wall speakers (Genelec / K-Array)
  • Multi-lingual audio trigger based on visitor badge selection

Technical FAQ

Experience Center Design FAQ

What is an Executive Briefing Center (EBC) or Experience Center?

An EBC is a specialized corporate environment designed to host high-value prospective clients, investors, and partners. It combines interactive storytelling displays, simulation zones, spatial audio, and luxury meeting spaces to showcase a company's innovation, vision, and market leadership.

How does curved MicroLED technology work in immersive tunnels?

Curved MicroLED (e.g., Samsung The Wall / LG MAGNIT) utilizes ultra-fine sub-millimeter pixel pitch modules engineered onto convex or concave structural frameworks. They deliver true black contrast, wide 160° viewing angles, and zero geometric distortion for walking tunnel experiences.

What is directional sound / spatial audio in experience centers?

Directional parametric speakers create narrow 'sound showers' focused specifically on a visitor standing in front of an exhibit, preventing audio bleed into adjacent demonstration pods without requiring headphones.

Can non-technical marketing teams easily update digital content?

Yes. We integrate enterprise CMS platforms (BrightSign Network, Samsung MagicINFO, or custom web portals) that allow marketing teams to update video reels, product spec sheets, and client welcome graphics remotely across all screens with a single click.

What interactive technologies are used on touch walls?

We deploy optical bonding Projected Capacitive (P-Cap) touch glass and laser infrared frames supporting 40+ simultaneous touch points, palm rejection, and physical fiducial object recognition.

Does GPSPL manage both AV hardware and custom structural mounting?

Yes, GPSPL provides complete turnkey execution—from initial spatial 3D architectural CAD layout, custom CNC-machined curved mounting structures, and electrical heat dissipation to final calibration and AMC support.
