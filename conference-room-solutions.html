Expert Engineering Insights FAQ

Frequently Asked Boardroom AV Questions

Clear architectural answers on acoustic treatment, ceiling beamforming vs table microphones, Microsoft Teams certification, and maintenance SLAs.

Why choose beamforming ceiling microphones over traditional table microphones?

Traditional table microphones clutter executive boardroom tables, invite unwanted paper rustling and laptop typing noise, and are frequently blocked by open laptops. Steerable ceiling arrays (such as Shure MXA920 or Sennheiser TeamConnect Ceiling 2) are flush-mounted overhead, completely eliminating table wires while using steerable acoustic lobes to pinpoint speaking executives with studio-grade clarity and automated noise cancellation.

What is required to achieve official Microsoft Teams Rooms (MTR) or Zoom Rooms certification?

Certified rooms require end-to-end hardware compliance: dedicated certified compute modules (Crestron Flex, HP Poly, or Lenovo ThinkSmart), certified beamforming microphone arrays with certified hardware AEC (Acoustic Echo Cancellation), certified AI tracking cameras, and certified touch controllers. GPSPL deploys only 100% native certified hardware combinations that pass Microsoft Teams Rooms audio/video telemetry standards without requiring driver workarounds.

How do you handle acoustic echo and glass-wall reverberation in modern corporate boardrooms?

Modern boardrooms often feature large glass windows and hard marble floors that reflect sound waves, causing hollow reverberation (RT60 > 1.0s). GPSPL resolves this through a dual approach: hardware acoustic signal processing (DSP with automated echo cancellation, adaptive noise suppression, and gain leveling) combined with discreet architectural acoustic wall panelling or micro-perforated acoustic treatments designed to match corporate interior aesthetics.

Should our boardroom deploy an Active MicroLED wall or dual commercial displays?

For boardrooms exceeding 8-10 meters in length or seating more than 16 executives, an Active MicroLED wall (110" to 165", 0.9mm pixel pitch) provides seamless bezel-free visuals, superior brightness (600-1000 nits), and true HDR contrast visible from every angle. For standard 12-16 seater rooms, dual 85-inch Sony or Samsung commercial displays offer excellent dual-stream capability (one screen for high-res presentation content, one for remote video participant gallery view).

What ongoing maintenance and warranty support does GPSPL provide?

Every turnkey deployment includes 1 year of comprehensive warranty backed directly by Tier-1 OEMs (Shure, Sennheiser, Crestron, Poly, Sony), plus GPSPL's Enterprise AMC SLA providing guaranteed 4-hour onsite response across Delhi NCR, bi-monthly proactive firmware audits, remote network telemetry health checks, and priority loaner equipment during unexpected hardware servicing.
