FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes the NU88C a 'Commercial' TV compared to residential models?

The LG NU88C includes customized commercial firmware (webOS 26) that supports **Hotel Mode** (USB cloning, channel lockout, start-volume cap, and custom welcome splash screen) and is built with durable components designed to run in continuous business environments.

Does it require a set-top box to stream LG Channels?

No! LG Channels stream free internet-based content directly. It requires only an internet connection (via Wi-Fi or LAN) and operates without any additional set-top boxes, subscriptions, or hidden charges.

Can we mirror mobile devices or laptops to the TV?

Yes, the NU88C commercial series has built-in support for Apple AirPlay and Google Cast, making screen mirroring extremely simple from both iOS/macOS and Android/ChromeOS platforms.
