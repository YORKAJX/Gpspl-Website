Enterprise & Education Interactive Display Guide

Interactive flat panels are replacing legacy projectors in classrooms and corporate boardrooms alike. Choosing the right interactive display solution requires balancing touch accuracy, operating systems, software ecosystems, and physical size. GPSPL coordinates the entire process, including selecting certified models like the interactive board for classroom or corporate huddle rooms, heavy-duty mobile carts or wall brackets, and complete classroom audio systems integration.

Choosing the Right Panel Size

Selecting the right size prevents user strain and ensures clear display details:

  • 65-Inch Displays: Ideal for smaller training labs, huddle spaces, and meeting rooms accommodating up to 6-8 participants.
  • 75-Inch Displays: Suitable for standard school classrooms and corporate conference rooms with 10-15 participants.
  • 86-Inch Displays: Recommended for large-format display needs, executive boardrooms, university lecture halls, and large training rooms.

Interactive Display FAQs

What is Android EDLA certification and why does it matter?

Enterprise Devices Licensing Agreement (EDLA) certification ensures the interactive panel has native access to Google Mobile Services (GMS), including Google Play Store, Google Workspace apps, and secure single sign-on (SSO), allowing teachers and corporate presenters to access files directly from the cloud securely.

What is the standard mounting height for an interactive board?

For corporate rooms, the center of the display is mounted between 55 to 60 inches from the floor for comfortable standing and viewing. In school classrooms, the display is mounted lower (45 to 50 inches from the floor) to allow younger students to reach the interactive board easily.

Can GPSPL configure active stylus integration and cloud sharing?

Yes. As a turnkey service provider, we configure display calibration, stylus write-delay optimization, dual-pen settings, screen mirroring apps (like LG Share), cloud remote manager, and local training room network integrations.

What maintenance is required for interactive displays?

Interactive panels require periodic screen cleaning with microfiber cloths, IR sensor bezel clearing, firmware updates, connectivity checks for OPS modules, and mounting structure safety audits, all covered under our AV AMC contracts.

Comparison Guide

Interactive Flat Panel vs. Classroom Projector

Determine the best interactive workspace solution for students and corporate training.

Feature Interactive Flat Panel (IFPD) Classroom Projector & Whiteboard
Writing & Touch Multi-touch (up to 50 points), active stylus writing, pinch-to-zoom. Static screen, no touch annotation (unless using costly interactive pens).
Presenter Shadow Zero shadow obstruction; presenter stands right at the board. Presenter blocks the light path, casting shadows and straining eyes.
Operating System Native Android (EDLA) with Google Workspace access and direct cloud save. Requires constant connection to an external laptop/PC via HDMI.
Consumables Maintenance Solid-state display, zero bulb or filter replacements needed. Requires periodic projection lamp replacements and filter cleaning.
