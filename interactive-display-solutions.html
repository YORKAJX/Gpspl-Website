Institutional FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Smart Boards

Key information regarding procurement, installation, warranties, and operating systems.

What is the price of a 4K Smart Board in India for 2026?
In India, commercial 4K smart boards range between ₹85,000 to ₹1,15,000 for 65-inch panels; ₹1,25,000 to ₹1,75,000 for 75-inch panels (the national K-12 standard); and ₹1,95,000 to ₹2,90,000 for 86-inch panels. Pricing varies based on brand (LG CreateBoard, Samsung, Maxhub, BenQ), whether Google EDLA certification is included, and if a Windows OPS PC module is added. GPSPL offers direct institutional wholesale pricing.
Should a school choose an Interactive Flat Panel or an interactive projector?
Interactive Flat Panels (IFPDs) have largely replaced projectors across Indian educational institutions. IFPDs offer 4K crystal-clear contrast without dimming lights, zero shadow casting from the teacher's hand, 50,000-hour LED panel lifespan without expensive lamp replacements, zero maintenance filters, and built-in Android/Windows whiteboard software.
What is Google EDLA certification and why is it essential?
Google EDLA (Enterprise Device Licensing Agreement) ensures official access to the Google Play Store, Google Workspace apps (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Classroom), and certified Google security patches. Non-EDLA panels require cumbersome APK side-loading and often face compatibility issues with modern classroom education apps.
Does GPSPL provide teacher software training and on-site warranty?
Yes. Every installation performed by GPSPL includes comprehensive faculty orientation on annotation software, cloud whiteboard saving, screen sharing workflows, and USB playback. All hardware carries a 3-Year Onsite OEM Commercial Warranty backed by GPSPL's 4-hour emergency SLA in Delhi NCR.
