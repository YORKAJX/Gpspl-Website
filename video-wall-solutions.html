Comparison Guide

LCD Video Wall vs. Active LED Video Wall

Choose the right multi-display technology for your command center or experience lobby.

Parameter LCD Video Wall Matrix Active LED Video Wall
Display Seams Visible bezels (0.88mm to 3.5mm grid lines across the image). 100% seamless display surface, zero grid lines or visual interruption.
Brightness & Color Fixed backlight (typically 500-700 nits), ideal for low-light text. Self-emissive LEDs, high brightness (600 to 1000+ nits), vibrant HDR colors.
Maintenance Mode Panel replacement is required for failures, causing matching batch issues. Front-serviceable module-level repair (swap individual magnetic modules in minutes).
Typical Application Control rooms, surveillance monitoring rooms, data analytics displays. Corporate lobbies, auditoriums, large conference rooms, high-impact signage.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about design, implementation, brands, support, and Pan-India project execution.

What type of video wall should we choose: LED or LCD?

The choice depends on viewing distance, brightness requirement, bezel tolerance, content type, budget, service access and operating hours. GPSPL studies these factors before recommending LED, LCD or a hybrid display approach.

Does GPSPL only install video walls or also supply products?

GPSPL is distribution-first. We can supply displays, LED cabinets, controllers, mounts, cables and related technology, and also provide design, integration, installation, commissioning and support.

Can GPSPL design video walls for control rooms?

Yes. GPSPL can plan display walls for command centers, network operations centers, surveillance rooms, emergency operations and other monitoring environments where reliability and multi-source visualization matter.

What information is needed for a video wall quote?

Useful inputs include wall size, viewing distance, room photos, content type, number of sources, required operating hours, indoor or outdoor use, brightness expectations, mounting surface and support requirements.

Does GPSPL provide calibration and commissioning?

Yes. GPSPL supports alignment, mapping, signal testing, brightness and color checks, commissioning, user training and handover documentation.

Can video walls be connected with AV switching and automation?

Yes. Video walls can be integrated with source switching, matrix routing, control panels, presentation systems, video conferencing, signage workflows and control-room operator workflows.

Does GPSPL provide AMC for video wall systems?

Yes. GPSPL can support preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, warranty coordination, spare planning and post-installation support through AMC and managed service options.
