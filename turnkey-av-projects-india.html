TURNKEY AV FAQ

Questions buyers ask before issuing an AV BOQ

What is included in a turnkey AV project?

It can include discovery, site assessment, design, BOQ planning, product supply, cabling and mounting coordination, configuration, testing, commissioning, user handover and support planning. The approved quotation defines the exact scope.

Does GPSPL handle corporate, education, hotel and religious AV projects?

GPSPL accepts enquiries for corporate collaboration spaces, education and training facilities, hospitality venues, religious spaces, retail and public-facing display systems. The recommendation depends on the site, audience and workflow.

Can GPSPL supply commercial TVs, LED walls and projectors with installation?

GPSPL can discuss commercial display, LED wall and projector supply with installation scope and project delivery. Model availability, pricing and installation terms are confirmed for the final requirement.
