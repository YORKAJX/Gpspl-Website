Common Queries

Delhi NCR AV Integration FAQs

Does GPSPL provide site surveys and consultations in Noida and Gurugram?

Yes. GPSPL is a leading AV integrator in Noida and Gurugram. We provide physical site surveys, display angle analysis, acoustic testing, and structured cabling planning for enterprise boardrooms, smart classrooms, and commercial spaces.

How does the GPSPL AV BOQ calculator help plan a conference room AV setup in Delhi NCR?

The AV BOQ calculator helps facility managers and IT teams plan display sizes, camera types, speaker coverage, control automation, and estimated budgets. Once you generate the planning BOQ, our Delhi NCR engineers coordinate a physical survey to finalize exact models and cabling requirements.

Does GPSPL coordinate warranty support and AMC services for global AV brands?

Yes, GPSPL coordinates warranty support directly with global technology partners like LG, Samsung, Sony, Crestron, ATEN, and Harman. We offer comprehensive AV AMC services with preventive maintenance visits, active LED wall calibration, and dedicated standby equipment.

What is included in the GPSPL AV commissioning and handover process?

Our commissioning process includes signal routing verification, DSP audio tuning for echo cancellation, LED display wall color calibration, and control panel automation testing. We provide complete layout drawings, system documentation, and user orientation sessions to ensure smooth operation.
