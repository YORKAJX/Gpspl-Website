Purpose-Built Features for Commercial Spaces

From standalone clinics to multi-branch retail networks, Samsung BEFX-H2 eliminates extra media players and complicated hardware.

Available on Android and iOS devices. Easily create custom promotional graphics, update pricing, and schedule auto-playback over local Wi-Fi without needing graphic designers.

Lock the TV interface with a secure master PIN code. Prevents customers or visitors from changing channels, adjusting volume limits, or inserting unauthorized USB flash drives.

Cloud-native content management and remote device monitoring. Update digital signage across 100+ locations simultaneously from a central browser console.
