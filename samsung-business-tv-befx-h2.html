Set a 4-digit master PIN that locks physical buttons, HDMI input switching, volume controls, and Wi-Fi access so public users cannot change channels or tamper with displays.

Samsung Business TV App

Choose from over 100 pre-loaded vertical and horizontal templates for food menus, retail sales discounts, clinic announcements, and event schedules with simple smartphone editing.

Automated On/Off Daily Timer

Program automatic operating hours (e.g. 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM). Displays turn on, boot to your branded promo channel automatically, and power off when the store closes.
