02. Quotations, Pricing & BOQ Estimation Disclaimers

All automated online estimators, catalog price indications, and preliminary BOQs generated via this website are indicative and intended exclusively for budgetary guidance:

  • Formal Validity: Binding commercial pricing is issued solely through formal, stamped quotation documents signed by an authorized GPSPL representative.
  • Validity Window: Standard quotations remain valid for 15 to 30 calendar days from issuance, subject to OEM hardware price adjustments and foreign exchange fluctuations for imported AV components.

03. Site Readiness & Project Execution Conditions

Successful AV integration and hardware commissioning depend on client site readiness as mutually agreed in the Scope of Work (SOW):

Standard Site Readiness Checklist: Physical civil/interior completion, dust-free environment for optical projectors/LED panels, dedicated 230V AC regulated UPS power sockets, functional conduit pathways, LAN drops/IP allocations, and core ceiling/wall mounting structural clearance.

Delays resulting from incomplete site readiness, civil obstructions, or non-availability of power/network infrastructure will extend handover timelines without imposing penalty liabilities on GPSPL.

04. Manufacturer Warranty & AMC Service Terms

GPSPL operates as an authorized distributor and certified system integrator:

  • OEM Manufacturer Warranty: Hardware products (displays, video walls, interactive panels, DSPs, cameras, microphones, UPS systems) carry standard manufacturer warranties as stipulated by OEM brands (typically 1 to 3 years). GPSPL facilitates technical RMA escalation, diagnosis, and manufacturer coordination.
  • GPSPL Workmanship Warranty: Cabling, crimping, physical mounting, rack dressing, and configuration implemented by GPSPL engineers carry a 90-day comprehensive workmanship warranty from sign-off.
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC): Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive AMC service level agreements (SLAs) are governed by dedicated service contracts specifying preventative visit frequencies, response times (4 to 24 hours), and standby unit provisioning.
