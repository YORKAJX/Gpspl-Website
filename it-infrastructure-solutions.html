Enterprise IT Infrastructure & AV Network Guide

Modern audio visual systems are network-dependent devices. From IP cameras and VoD encoders to network-managed DSPs and active LED walls, standard IT hardware must support high-bandwidth traffic. GPSPL specializes in IT infrastructure solutions, integrating servers, storage, enterprise switches, online UPS power backup, network rack layouts, and structured cabling to guarantee low-latency AV network readiness.

Core Deliverables in IT & Cable Integration

Our IT infrastructure division covers:

  • AV Network Readiness: Designing Layer 2/3 IGMP switching networks to prevent multicast audio-video stream lag.
  • Server & Storage Sourcing: Supplying enterprise servers and storage platforms for security monitoring and database operations.
  • Rack and Cabling: Designing clean server racks with vertical and horizontal managers, and Cat6A/Fibre structured cabling.
  • Online UPS Systems: Sourcing high-capacity double-conversion online UPS systems to isolate critical AV/IT nodes from power drops.

IT Infrastructure FAQs

Why is AV network readiness testing critical before installation?

AV-over-IP streams send massive amounts of uncompressed multicast data. If the local IT network has not been configured with IGMP snooping, QoS prioritization, and appropriate VLAN segmentation, it can cause severe packet loss, audio sync lag, or complete switch crashes.

What structured cabling standards does GPSPL follow?

We strictly adhere to TIA/EIA-568-C standards, using certified Cat6A shielded cables for AV-over-IP and HDBaseT runs to isolate electromagnetic interference. Every line is terminated, combed, labeled, and tested using professional Fluke analyzers.

Does GPSPL offer IT AMC and SLA support in Delhi NCR?

Yes. We provide complete IT infrastructure AMC support, covering switch monitoring, network cable audits, UPS battery load tests, cabinet cleaning, hardware diagnostics, and warranty coordination with IT brands.

What online UPS capacity is needed for a standard server rack?

Capacity depends on the total active load (servers, switches, routers). Typically, a 3kVA to 6kVA online double-conversion UPS is standard for a single rack to maintain operations and isolate the hardware from voltage transients.

Buying Guidance

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these frequent pitfalls when planning, sourcing, and installing enterprise technology systems.

01

Unmanaged Switches Sourcing

Routing high-bandwidth AV over IP multicast traffic over unmanaged network switches, causing network lag.

02

Non-Shielded Cable Routing

Sourcing non-shielded Cat6 lines, leading to electromagnetic signal interference in data lines.

03

Skipping Cable Labeling

Failing to tag both ends of routing lines, making future debugging tedious.

04

No Storage RAID Planning

Running database storage servers without calculating redundancy RAID margins, risking data loss.
