Category overview

What GPSPL can supply in this technology category.

Modern AV rooms combine displays, microphones, cameras, speakers, DSP, processors, source devices, laptops, lighting, conferencing platforms and network infrastructure. GPSPL designs control and automation workflows so users can start meetings, switch sources, control displays, route audio/video, operate scenes and get support without dealing with disconnected equipment.
