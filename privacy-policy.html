Privacy Policy

How Global Peripheral Solution Pvt. Ltd. handles enquiry and contact information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

When you submit a website form or contact GPSPL, we may collect your name, company or organization, email address, phone number, project location, requirement type and message details.

How We Use This Information

We use enquiry information to respond to your requirement, understand project scope, prepare quotations or consultation responses, coordinate support and maintain business communication records.

Sharing and Storage

Website enquiries may be delivered to GPSPL business email accounts and service providers used for form delivery. We do not sell enquiry information. Access is limited to team members handling sales, support or project communication.

This website may link to WhatsApp, Google Maps, social media platforms and third-party form delivery services. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies.
