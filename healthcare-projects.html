Case Study Proof

Healthcare AV & Medical Training Setup Case Study

Project: Live Surgery Auditory & Visual Conferencing Setup

Client Context:

A multi-specialty hospital and medical research clinic in Delhi NCR required a live surgery broadcasting system to transmit high-resolution camera feeds from operation theaters (OT) to academic auditoriums.

Business Requirement:

Provide medical-grade 4K camera routing with zero compression delay. Ensure absolute audio clarity so surgeons can describe procedures to training students without losing focus, and establish two-way audio conferencing between the surgeon and auditorium moderators.

Supplied Equipment:

Sony 4K Surgical PTZ Cameras, Lumens encoders/decoders for AV-over-IP routing, Sennheiser hands-free surgical headset microphones, Harman JBL tuned conference speakers, and Crestron matrix visual routing boards.

Delivery & Commissioning Method:

GPSPL deployed dust-sealed cabling and mounted medical-grade camera brackets in the OT. We optimized network switches for zero-latency multicast routing, configured anti-feedback audio filters on the surgical headsets, and tested remote camera pan/zoom parameters from the auditorium console.

Support Outcome:

Broadcasted over 150 successful live surgeries to student batches. Handed over detailed cabling layout diagrams to the hospital's IT department. Supported under a specialized SLA AMC contract covering quarterly medical display audits and backup encoder standby logistics.
