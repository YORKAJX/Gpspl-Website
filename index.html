We listen first. Site brief, room function, stakeholder expectations and budget parameters are captured before any design begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before planning a project?

Start with the most common questions about AV BOQ planning, room setup, installation, pricing validation and support.

Can GPSPL prepare an AV BOQ before a site survey?

Yes. The AV BOQ calculator gives a room-wise planning estimate using room type, size, seating and workflow. Final model, cable route, mounting, exact quantity and commercials are confirmed after site validation.

What is included in a conference room AV setup?

A typical setup can include display or LED wall, camera, microphones, speakers, DSP, amplifier, touch control, scheduler, rack, UPS, cabling, installation, programming, testing and user handover.

Does GPSPL provide installation and commissioning?

Yes. GPSPL supports product supply, rack assembly, mounting, cable laying, termination, configuration, DSP/control programming, testing, commissioning, documentation and handover where applicable.

Does the BOQ calculator show final pricing?

No. It shows a GST-inclusive planning range. Final pricing may vary after equipment selection, site survey, cable route, installation complexity, warranty requirement and client approval.

Can GPSPL work with existing AV or IT infrastructure?

Yes. GPSPL can assess existing displays, audio, network, rack, power and cabling, then recommend what can be reused, upgraded or replaced for reliable performance.

How do I request a formal quotation?

You can build an AV BOQ, download the proposal, share room details, or contact GPSPL directly. The team will review the requirement and prepare the formal quote after technical validation.
