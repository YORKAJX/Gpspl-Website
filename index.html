      How We Work

      Your project, engineered from first call to final handover.

      Every GPSPL engagement follows a structured 7-phase process that eliminates surprises, protects your budget and delivers systems that perform on day one.

      01

      Requirement Discovery

      We listen first. Site brief, room function, stakeholder expectations and budget parameters are captured before any design begins.

      02

      Site Survey & Acoustic Analysis

      Physical site visit to measure room dimensions, ceiling height, reverberation, ambient noise and infrastructure readiness.

      03

      System Design & BOQ

      Signal flow diagrams, speaker placement calculations, display sizing, rack design and a complete itemised BOQ with GST-inclusive pricing.

      04

      Procurement & Staging

      OEM-authorised procurement, goods received at GPSPL warehouse, pre-staging and firmware updates before any equipment reaches site.

      05

      Installation & Integration

      Professional installation by GPSPL-certified engineers. Structured cabling, mounting, rack assembly and system integration on schedule.

      06

      Commissioning & Tuning

      Full system commissioning — DSP programming, display calibration, camera tracking setup, control system configuration and acoustic tuning.

      07

      Handover & AMC Support

      User training, as-built documentation, warranty registration and AMC contract activation. Your system is supported for years, not just days.
