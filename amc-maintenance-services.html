Contract FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About AV Maintenance

Key information regarding coverage, transition process, SLAs, and emergency callouts.

How does the onboarding and site takeover process work?
Our onboarding begins with a zero-cost physical site health audit. A senior GPSPL engineer inspects all displays, cable terminations, firmware versions, and rack thermal parameters, producing an asset register and remedial repair roadmap before formal contract commencement.
Are emergency breakdown visits capped or charged extra?
No. All GPSPL Gold and Platinum AMC agreements include unlimited emergency breakdown callouts. You never pay unexpected technician dispatch fees when equipment fails during critical business hours.
Can GPSPL coordinate repairs for products still under OEM factory warranty?
Yes. As an authorized distributor and system integrator, we handle complete warranty lifecycle management. We generate official brand RMA tickets, manage direct factory service centers for Samsung, LG, Poly, Shure, and Crestron, and re-install tested units without bothering your internal IT teams.
Do you provide resident AV engineers for large enterprise campuses?
Yes. Under our Platinum SLA tier, we depute factory-certified Resident AV Engineers stationed directly at your corporate headquarters or campus. They manage daily meeting room readiness, provide executive meeting escort, and resolve minor user queries instantly.
