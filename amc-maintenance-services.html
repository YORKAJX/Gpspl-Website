Enterprise AV AMC & Maintenance Services Guide

Having a state-of-the-art conference room or digital display wall is only beneficial if it remains operational. System drift, loose cables, dust accumulation in project ventilation, and un-updated firmware can disrupt critical meetings. Setting up a dedicated AV AMC service or structured AV maintenance contract protects your technology investment. GPSPL handles lifecycle support—from initial installation and commissioning to regular preventive audits, spare parts coordination, and on-site troubleshooting.

Key Advantages of GPSPL SLA Contracts

Our SLA contracts are structured to keep systems up and running:

  • Preventive Audits: Quarterly health checkups to test visual signaling, adjust microphone echo cancellation, and measure UPS battery health.
  • Dedicated Spares: We inventory critical standby displays, cables, and converters to ensure rapid swap-outs during hardware failures.
  • Direct OEM Support: Direct channels for factory-certified warranty coordination with partners like LG, Samsung, Sony, Poly, and Crestron.

AV AMC & Support FAQs

What is included in a standard AV AMC contract with GPSPL?

Our standard AMC covers scheduled quarterly preventive maintenance visits, on-site technical support for device issues, display calibration, cable integrity testing, audio echo cancellation fine-tuning, firmware updates, and direct coordination for manufacturer warranty claims.

Do you support existing AV systems installed by other integrators?

Yes. We can assess already-installed systems in Delhi NCR. Our engineers run a physical audit of the cabling, displays, controllers, and racks to evaluate the current system health and draft a custom AMC coverage plan.

How quickly can GPSPL dispatch an engineer for on-site troubleshooting?

Within Delhi NCR, we offer guaranteed response windows (from 24 to 48 hours depending on your chosen SLA tier). Our localized service hubs in Nehru Place, Noida, and Gurugram ensure prompt dispatch of certified technicians.

How does GPSPL handle hardware replacement during warranty periods?

For systems under active manufacturer warranty, we coordinate log creation, ship faulty hardware to authorized brand service centers, oversee repair timelines, and remount the serviced unit at your site, ensuring zero hassle for your IT teams.
