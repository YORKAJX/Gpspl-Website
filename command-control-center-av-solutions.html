Technical Clarifications

Control Room Integration FAQ

What display technologies are best for 24/7 Command & Control Centers?

For 24/7 mission-critical environments, Direct-View Fine-Pitch Active LED (P0.9 to P1.56) and Ultra-Narrow Bezel (0.88mm) Commercial LCD Video Walls from Samsung, LG, and Barco are standard. They provide 100,000-hour MTBF lifespans, redundant power supplies, and zero burn-in risk.

How does KVM-over-IP matrix switching benefit SOC and NOC operators?

KVM-over-IP allows operators to control dozens of secure remote workstations, CCTV servers, and SCADA systems using a single keyboard and mouse across multiple monitors without physical workstation heat and noise at operator desks.

How does GPSPL handle redundancy and failover in control rooms?

We engineer N+1 hot-swappable power supplies, redundant dual-loop network cabling, dual video processors with instant auto-switchover, and separate uninterruptible power supply (UPS) circuits to ensure 99.999% continuous operation.

What is ISO 11064 compliance in control room design?

ISO 11064 defines ergonomic standards for control centers, including optimal operator sightlines (under 30° vertical neck tilt), acoustic ambient noise control (under 45 dBA), thermal heat dissipation, and motorized sit-stand console arrangements to reduce operator fatigue.

Are your solutions compliant with GeM Government tenders in India?

Yes, GPSPL is an authorized GeM (Government e-Marketplace) registered vendor with complete OEM Manufacturer Authorization Forms (MAF), BIS certifications, and Make-in-India (MII) compliance documentation for public sector and ministry procurements.

Does GPSPL provide 24/7 on-site AMC support for control rooms in Delhi NCR?
