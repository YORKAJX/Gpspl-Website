Installation Notes

Recommended orientation: Keep the display and podium on the front 30 ft wall. Place the AV rack close to the podium side but not blocking the audience path. Speaker points should be adjusted after checking ceiling type, false ceiling access, beam positions and final seating layout.

Client approval points: Final rack location, display mounting height, conduit route, speaker mounting type, power socket location and podium position should be frozen before installation starts.
