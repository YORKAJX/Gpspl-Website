Bangalore Delivery & Engineering SLA Model

For Bangalore enterprise clients, GPSPL operates on a scheduled turnkey project delivery model. Hardware (Samsung, LG, Harman, Shure, Crestron, Barco) is supplied directly from authorized distribution channels with verified manufacturer warranties, followed by dispatched senior deployment engineers who execute CAD cabling schematics, rack dressing, Dante audio calibration, and user training on site. Post-commissioning support is managed via remote CMS administration and scheduled preventive AMC maintenance.
