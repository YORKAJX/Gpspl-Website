## GEO (AI search) audit

GEO is not a separate Google ranking switch. Gemini and other AI search experiences need accessible, corroborated facts. The strongest inputs are:

1. One consistent business identity: exact name, address, phone, hours and social profiles across website, Google Business Profile, OEM pages and directories.
2. Named, permissioned project evidence: client/sector, city, scope, date, photos and measurable outcome. Do not invent client names, review text, deployment counts, certifications or OEM authorisation.
3. Clear, concise answer sections: what is included, who it is for, technical decisions and buyer checklists. Service and FAQ structured data should match visible content.
4. Independent corroboration: genuine Google reviews, approved OEM partner listings, trade mentions and client backlinks.

## Risks to resolve before publishing more pages

- Several phone numbers appear across the website. Confirm which number is sales, technical support and Google Business Profile primary number; keep each use consistent.
- The site contains rating/review schema. Retain it only if the displayed count, rating and review text are verifiable and match the corresponding public source. Google does not award self-serving review rich results for a business's own services.
- Verify every `authorised`, `certified`, SLA, project-count, stock and pricing claim against source records before using it in schema or page copy.
- Do not create hundreds of city-keyword doorway pages. Use genuine local project proof and a city page only when the content differs materially.
