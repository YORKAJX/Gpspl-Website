Why Online UPS is Critical for AV Racks

Offline or line-interactive UPS systems are insufficient for sensitive pro AV systems:

  • Zero Transfer Time: Online UPS runs the active load continuously off the battery bank, meaning there is zero delay during utility power drop.
  • Pure Sine Wave Output: Eliminates electrical noise and hum from audio amplifiers, speakers, and digital video streams.
  • Voltage Regulation: Corrects brownouts and high-voltage surges continuously, extending AV processor lifespans.

UPS Power Backup FAQs

What is the difference between online and offline UPS systems?

Offline UPS passes utility power directly to devices and switches to batteries in 6-10ms when power fails. Online UPS continuously converts incoming AC to DC, and back to clean AC, isolating devices from line noise and offering zero-millisecond transfer delay.

How do we calculate the battery backup time needed for a room?

Battery backup is calculated by adding the total power consumption (in Watts) of the connected hardware and matching it with the battery Ampere-Hour (AH) capacity. GPSPL provides load-matching calculations to support 30-minute, 1-hour, or longer backup windows.

Does GPSPL handle battery health testing and replacement under AMC?

Yes. Under our AMC program, our field engineers perform quarterly battery terminal checks, voltage load tests, battery cell replacement coordination, and remote SNMP monitoring setup.

Comparison Guide

Online Double-Conversion vs. Offline UPS

Understand power protection differences for sensitive AV racks and IT equipment.

Parameter Online Double-Conversion UPS Offline / Line-Interactive UPS
Transfer Delay 0 milliseconds. Load runs off battery loop continuously. 6 to 10 milliseconds delay during utility power failure.
Sine Wave Quality Pure sine wave output. Eliminates audio buzz and video lines. Simulated sine wave. Can introduce audible hum and screen noise.
Voltage Regulation Continuous voltage regulation, filtering surges, drops, and noise. Switches to battery only on extreme spikes, leaving drops unregulated.
Best For Professional AV racks, active LED displays, core servers, DSPs. Basic desktop PCs, printers, and non-critical network routers.

Buying Guidance

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these frequent pitfalls when planning, sourcing, and installing enterprise technology systems.

01

Purchasing Offline UPS

Using offline UPS lines with transfer delays, causing server reboots during power brownouts.

02

Simulated Sine Wave Noise

Buying simulated sine wave UPS units that introduce audio hums into pro amplifiers.

03

Ignoring Battery Venting

Failing to plan for room temperature control, shortening battery backup lifespan.

04

Skipping Maintenance Bypass Switch

Sourcing rack UPS systems without a manual bypass switch, requiring downtime during battery replacement.
