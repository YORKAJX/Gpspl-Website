About Global Peripheral Solution Pvt. Ltd.

Delhi NCR AV system integration for rooms, campuses and enterprise facilities.

GPSPL began in 1997 with a distribution-led foundation and has grown into a practical technology partner for organizations that need more than a product quote. The company combines product availability, brand coordination, room-aware solution planning, installation quality and after-sales accountability.

Today, GPSPL supports conference rooms, boardrooms, huddle spaces, classrooms, training rooms, auditoriums, command spaces, digital signage networks, hospitality venues, healthcare spaces, government facilities and enterprise IT environments.

The goal is simple: recommend the right technology, install it cleanly, document it clearly and keep the client supported after handover.

Company Journey

From peripheral supply to complete AV and technology integration.

A practical evolution shaped by enterprise procurement, display systems, smart classrooms, professional audio and long-term service support.

1997 – 2000

Started as a peripheral solutions provider focusing on HP and Epson printers, cartridges, toners, computing peripherals and institutional IT supply.

2001 – 2008

Expanded into server infrastructure, storage solutions, networking, and security systems to help organizations build reliable IT environments.

2009 – 2010

Entered the display solutions market with Samsung LFD (Large Format Display) deployments.

2010 – 2015

Diversified into video solutions, IT infrastructure, and networking technologies.

2015 Onwards

Expanded into professional audio visual solutions, JBL systems, and large-screen display technologies.

2015 – 2018

Successfully introduced smart classroom solutions for educational institutions.

2018 – 2022

Focused on hybrid classroom technologies that enable modern and flexible learning environments.

2023 – Present

Delivering professional audio visual solutions for lecture halls, auditoriums, meeting rooms, boardrooms, training centers, and enterprise collaboration spaces.

Welcome to GPSPL.

When GPSPL started in 1997, our first responsibility was to earn trust through dependable technology supply. Over the years, the market changed from individual products to complete connected environments: meeting rooms, classrooms, auditoriums, LED walls, professional audio systems, automation, IT infrastructure and managed support.

Our direction remains clear. We want every client to receive a solution that is technically suitable, commercially sensible and easy to maintain after installation. A successful project is not only one that looks good on delivery day; it is one that keeps working reliably for the people who use it every day.

As we move forward, GPSPL will continue to invest in better solution planning, stronger brand partnerships, trained teams, cleaner execution and faster support. We are grateful to our clients, partners and employees for being part of this journey, and we remain committed to building technology environments that are practical, future-ready and accountable.

- Mr. Sanjay Khurana Founder & Director, GPSPL
