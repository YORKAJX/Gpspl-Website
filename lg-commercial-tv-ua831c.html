FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact physical depth of the UA831C display?

The display features a streamlined depth measuring exactly **67.9 mm** (across all screen sizes: 43/50/55/65 inch displays), allowing it to blend seamlessly into walls and panels.

Does it support AirPlay and Google Cast mirroring?

Yes! The UA831C series features built-in support for both Apple AirPlay and Google Cast, letting presenters mirror content effortlessly from any iPhone, Android phone, or laptop.

Which HDMI port supports the eARC return loop?

HDMI port number **2** is eARC enabled, allowing the display to route lossless digital audio signals back to external Soundbars or DSP mixers.
