What should I share for a conference room or boardroom enquiry?

Share room size, seating capacity, display requirement, video conferencing platform, microphone preference, and whether automation or touch control is needed.

Yes. GPSPL can help with maintenance, warranty extension, health checks, system upgrades, and after-sales support for installed AV and IT systems.
