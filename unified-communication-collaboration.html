Comparison Guide

Integrated Video Bar vs. Modular PTZ Camera System

Select the optimal video collaboration design for your meeting room size.

Parameter Integrated Video Bar Modular PTZ Camera System
Room Suitability Small to medium rooms (up to 8-10 participants, 15ft length). Large boardrooms, training classrooms, auditoriums, and flexible halls.
Component Density All-in-one chassis (camera, mic array, and speaker integrated). Separate PTZ cameras, ceiling mic arrays, standalone DSP, and speakers.
Installation Effort Simple mounting above/below screen; single USB connection. Structured under-table/ceiling cabling, AV rack, and DSP calibration.
Expandability Typically limited to daisy-chaining one external table mic. Supports multi-camera switching, ceiling mic networks, and high-power audio.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about design, implementation, brands, support, and Pan-India project execution.

What is Unified Communication & Collaboration?

Unified Communication & Collaboration combines video conferencing, meeting-room audio, cameras, displays, presentation sharing, room control and IT readiness into one reliable communication environment.

Does GPSPL only install video conferencing equipment?

No. GPSPL supports product supply, solution design, integration, commissioning, training, warranty coordination, AMC and after-sales support for complete collaboration environments.

Which spaces can GPSPL design for collaboration?

GPSPL can support boardrooms, conference rooms, huddle rooms, training centers, smart classrooms, hybrid learning spaces, healthcare communication rooms, government facilities and enterprise campuses.

Which brands does GPSPL work with for UC solutions?

GPSPL works with enterprise technology portfolios including HP Poly, Sony, Samsung, MAXHUB, Newline, BenQ, Harman Professional, JBL Professional, Crestron, AMX, ATEN, Lumens, LG Business Solutions and other project-fit technologies.

Can GPSPL help with product selection before purchase?

Yes. GPSPL can help evaluate room size, participants, camera coverage, microphone pickup, display size, connectivity, platform requirements, budget and future support needs before preparing a solution.

Do you support Pan-India project execution?

Yes. GPSPL can help standardize room types, product stacks, deployment methods and support planning for organizations with multiple branches, campuses or offices.

Do you provide AMC for meeting-room systems?

Yes. GPSPL can provide AMC, preventive maintenance, technical troubleshooting, warranty guidance, support visits and upgrade planning based on project scope.

Can existing meeting rooms be upgraded?

Yes. GPSPL can assess existing displays, cameras, microphones, cabling, network readiness and room usage to recommend practical upgrades where technically feasible.
