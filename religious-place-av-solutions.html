Can GPSPL integrate sound, lighting and AV in an existing dome?

Yes. For an existing domed prayer hall, auditorium or event venue, GPSPL can scope speech and music coverage, lighting zones, video displays and controls. Share the dome diameter and height, floor plan, audience positions and photos of the existing equipment.

For a sound upgrade, identify the seats where speech is difficult to follow. For lighting, describe the areas that need independent control during services or events. Proposed mounts, cable routes and access for maintenance need review with the venue team before installation.
