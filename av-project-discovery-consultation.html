Real Engineering Methodology

How Our Engineers Execute Your Project from Day 1

We do not sell boxed products and walk away. Every installation follows a rigorous 6-stage engineering lifecycle to ensure zero downtime and peak performance.

01

Spatial & Acoustic Room Audit

Our field engineers visit your site with sound level meters and laser distometers. In large halls or mandirs, we measure RT60 reverberation times, ambient background noise (NC rating), and structural wall load capacity before designing the equipment layout.

02

3D CAD Schematics & Cable Pathways

We generate detailed AutoCAD conduit layouts, single-line diagrams (SLD), and rack elevation drawings. Power circuit requirements, heat dissipation (BTU/hr), and line-of-sight angles are fully planned before interior civil work finishes.

03

Factory Burn-In & Staging Quality Check

Hardware sourced directly from our OEM partners (Samsung, LG, Harman, Shure, Crestron) undergoes 48-hour continuous burn-in testing in our staging lab. Defective modules or firmware issues are caught in the warehouse, not on your site.

04

Precision Rigging & Structured Cabling

Our certified technicians pull LSZH fire-rated Cat6A/fiber lines through dedicated conduits away from high-voltage cables to prevent EM interference. Displays are laser-leveled with heavy-duty structural unistrut mounting.

05

DSP Notch Tuning & Optical Calibration

Using calibrated measurement microphones and DSP software (BSS Soundweb / Biamp / Symetrix), we tune parametric EQ filters to eliminate room resonance and feedback squeal. Video walls undergo seam-level optical color balancing.

06

Handover, Training & 4-Hour On-Site SLA

We provide as-built drawings, cable labeling maps, and on-site staff training. Your installation is backed by GPSPL's direct AMC with a guaranteed 4-hour field engineer response time across Delhi NCR and standby spare inventory.
