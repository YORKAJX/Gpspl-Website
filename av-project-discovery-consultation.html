Practical Engineering

How Our Engineers Execute Your Project from Start to Finish

We do not just ship boxed hardware. Every project follows a disciplined engineering workflow to guarantee reliability, zero downtime, and clean aesthetics.

Step 01

Site Survey & Acoustic / Lux Measurement

Our field engineers inspect your site with laser distometers and sound meters. We check room dimensions, ambient daylight (lux levels), wall load capacity, and RT60 reverberation times.

Step 02

3D CAD Layout & Conduit Pathways

We prepare detailed AutoCAD conduit layouts, single-line wiring diagrams, and rack elevations. Conduit sizing and heat dissipation are finalized before civil finishes commence.

Step 03

OEM Sourcing & 48-Hour Staging Burn-In

Hardware sourced directly from our tier-1 OEM partners (Samsung, LG, Harman, Shure) is tested in our staging facility for 48 hours continuously to catch any defects before reaching your site.

Step 04

Laser Mounting & Structured Cabling

Our certified technicians install fire-rated LSZH cabling through segregated conduits away from high-voltage cables. Displays and line arrays are laser-aligned with heavy-duty structural mounts.

Step 05

DSP Notch Tuning & Optical Seam Calibration

Using measurement microphones and DSP software (BSS/Biamp), we eliminate audio feedback squeal. Video walls undergo module-by-module optical color and brightness balancing.

Step 06

Handover, Training & 4-Hour On-Site SLA

We supply as-built schematics, train your team on daily operations, and back your setup with our 4-hour on-site maintenance SLA and dedicated local spare parts inventory.
