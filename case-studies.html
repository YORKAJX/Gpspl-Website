Our 4-Phase Engineering Process

Why India's top corporate, education, and hospitality organizations trust GPSPL from initial blueprint to long-term lifecycle AMC.

STAGE 01

Acoustic & Sightline Survey

Comprehensive on-site evaluation: room dimensions, reverberation time (RT60), ambient lux levels, viewing angles, and conduit pathways.

STAGE 02

Custom BOQ & Schematics

Engineering detailed single-line wiring diagrams (SLDs), rack layout plans, equipment compatibility validation, and transparent BOQs.

STAGE 03

Turnkey Integration & DSP Tuning

Precision rack dressing, physical mounting, firmware synchronization, DSP acoustic echo cancellation (AEC) calibration, and user training.

STAGE 04

Lifecycle AMC & SLA Support

Dedicated AMC support contracts with preventative quarterly maintenance, guaranteed 4-hour SLA response, and standby unit provisioning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Case Study & Project Integration Insights

Key technical and commercial queries regarding GPSPL project execution across India.

What is included in a GPSPL AV project case study?

Every GPSPL case study details the initial client challenge, room acoustic & architectural constraints, supplied OEM hardware BOQ, installation & commissioning scope, and measurable business outcomes such as uptime SLAs and user adoption metrics.

How does GPSPL calculate the BOQ for a 20-person hybrid boardroom?

GPSPL calculates the BOQ based on room dimensions, ceiling height, viewing distance formulas (DISCAS 4K standards), beamforming microphone coverage radiuses, PoE switching requirements, and platform ecosystem preferences (Microsoft Teams Rooms / Zoom Rooms / BYOD).

Can GPSPL integrate new AV hardware with existing IT infrastructure?

Yes. Our certified engineers assess existing displays, legacy amplifiers, rack cabinets, and LAN switches to integrate modern AV-over-IP, DSP processors, and touch panel automation seamlessly without redundant capital expenditure.

What warranty and AMC support SLAs does GPSPL provide after deployment?

GPSPL provides full OEM manufacturer warranty backing (1-3 years), a 90-day installation workmanship warranty, and customizable Comprehensive/Non-Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) with guaranteed 4 to 24-hour SLA response times in Delhi NCR.
