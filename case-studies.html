Technology Requirements GPSPL Can Handle

These examples show common project situations GPSPL supports across corporate, education, display, control-room, signage and AMC requirements.

Boardroom

Hybrid Meeting Room Upgrade

Challenge: unclear audio and complex meeting setup.
Solution: display, PTZ camera, microphones, speakers, source switching and room control.
Result: easier meetings and support-ready documentation.

Education

Smart Classroom Rollout

Challenge: traditional classrooms needed interactive teaching tools.
Solution: interactive panels, classroom audio, Type-C connectivity, projection options and handover.
Result: more engaging teaching and simpler operation.

Display

Active LED Visual Experience

Challenge: large-format visual communication for a high-impact space.
Solution: LED cabinets, controllers, structure, calibration and AMC planning.
Result: reliable display experience with service support.

Control Room

Video Wall & KVM Workflow

Challenge: multiple sources and operators needed clean routing.
Solution: video wall, KVM, AV switching, rack planning, cabling and testing.
Result: better visibility and maintainable signal flow.

Support

AMC for Enterprise AV Systems

Challenge: recurring maintenance and support gaps after installation.
Solution: preventive checks, response planning, warranty coordination and reporting.
Result: better uptime and one accountable support route.

Signage

Digital Signage Network

Challenge: consistent communication across public or enterprise spaces.
Solution: commercial displays, media players, mounting, content workflow and service planning.
Result: scalable visual communication.
