Frequently Asked Questions

Coworking AV & Video Conferencing FAQs

What AV solutions are essential for modern coworking spaces in India?

The core AV infrastructure for high-performing coworking spaces includes: 1) One-touch native Microsoft Teams & Zoom Rooms for 4-to-12 seater meeting rooms; 2) Wireless BYOD presentation systems (Barco ClickShare / Kramer VIA) that work without drivers on Windows and Mac; 3) Acoustic calling booth/pod AV setups; 4) Wall-mounted PoE room scheduling panels showing live meeting status; 5) Active LED video walls and portable line array sound for community town halls and member networking events.

Why is BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) wireless screen sharing crucial for shared offices?

In a coworking environment, hundreds of different members and guests connect daily using diverse laptops (MacBooks, ThinkPads, Surface, Chromebooks). Cable-only setups lead to constant HDMI adapter failures and member frustration. GPSPL installs enterprise wireless presentation gateways (Barco ClickShare, Kramer VIA, Mersive) allowing instant 4K wireless casting via AirPlay, Miracast, Google Cast, or USB-C dongles in under 5 seconds with zero software installation.

Can GPSPL integrate room booking touch panels outside meeting rooms?

Yes. We deploy sleek 7-inch to 10-inch PoE touch panels from Crestron, Yealink, and Logitech that mount directly onto glass or drywall outside each meeting room. They sync in real-time with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or coworking management software (Cobot, Nexudus, OfficeRnD). Vibrant LED side bars glow green when free and red when occupied, eliminating meeting interruptions.

What SLA and maintenance support does GPSPL offer for multi-location coworking operators?

GPSPL provides comprehensive Pan-India AMC and SLA support with a guaranteed 4-hour on-site response in major metro clusters (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad). Our services include scheduled quarterly preventive maintenance, remote cloud health monitoring, and standby equipment buffer to ensure zero downtime for paying workspace tenants.
