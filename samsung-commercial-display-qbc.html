Samsung QBC Architectural Highlights

Designed for modern corporate aesthetics, institutional corridors, retail show windows, and premium interactive meeting spaces.

Ultra-Slim 28.5mm Depth

Unprecedented slimness allows the QBC series to hang almost completely flush against walls, creating an elegant architectural showcase in executive offices and gallery spaces.

Dynamic Crystal Color (1B Colors)

Experience pure, realistic color transitions. 10-bit color depth reproduces every subtle shade with breathtaking accuracy for luxury brand marketing and high-definition presentations.

Centered VESA & Even Bezels

Symmetrical bezels and centralized VESA mounting holes ensure perfectly balanced weight distribution, preventing screen tilting when rotating between portrait and landscape modes.

350 Nits Non-Glare Panel

Optimized for indoor office lighting, retail corridors, and training rooms. Reduces glare without washing out deep blacks and vibrant colors.

Tizen 7.0 Smart Signage Platform

High-performance SOC with 16GB flash storage. Run complex web-based signage apps and HTML5 content without needing an external PC or Android stick.

Samsung VXT & MagicINFO S10

Comprehensive remote CMS compatibility. Create, schedule, deploy, and monitor promo content across single displays or national multi-city retail networks.
