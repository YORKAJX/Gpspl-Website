Why Choose the Samsung QBC Signage Series?

Engineered for retail stores, corporate reception hubs, healthcare corridors, and hospitality venues.

Ultra Slim 28.5mm Depth

With a depth of only 28.5mm, the display blends cleanly into any interior design without protruding into walkways or corridors.

Dynamic Crystal Color

1 billion shades of color ensure every product graphic, video advertisement, and corporate brand asset renders with true-to-life accuracy.

Portrait & Landscape Orientation

Even bezels on all 4 sides and centered VESA mounting holes allow smooth 90-degree vertical portrait totems or standard landscape mounting.

Built-in Cloud Workspace

Direct access to Microsoft 365, VMware Horizon, and Windows RDP remote desktops directly on the display without connecting an external PC.
