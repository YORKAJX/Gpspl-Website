Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent technical, commercial, and warranty insights to assist your procurement team.

What is the typical cost per sq ft for commercial Active LED video walls in India?

Commercial Active LED wall pricing in India generally ranges from ₹3,500 to ₹18,000+ per sq ft depending on pixel pitch (P0.9 MicroLED to P3.9 Outdoor), packaging technology (COB vs SMD), refresh rate (3,840Hz broadcast vs 1,920Hz), and processor grade (NovaStar COEX/VX series). As a direct Tier-1 authorized distributor for Sony, Newline, Samsung, LG, and Absen, GPSPL provides direct wholesale OEM pricing with zero intermediary markups.

What is the key difference between Chip-on-Board (COB) and Surface Mount Device (SMD)?

COB (Chip-on-Board) mounts LED flip-chips directly onto the PCB substrate and covers them with an optically transparent epoxy resin. This delivers IP54 front protection against dust, water droplets, and physical impact, wider 170° viewing angles, and zero dead lamp beads caused by accidental touching. SMD (Surface Mount Device) solders individual packaged diodes onto the board, making it highly cost-effective for larger auditoriums and stage setups where viewers sit more than 3 meters away.

Can GPSPL provide Manufacturer Authorization Format (MAF) letters for GeM and government tenders?

Yes. GPSPL is a registered GeM (Government e-Marketplace) OEM partner and authorized Tier-1 enterprise distributor. We provide authentic Manufacturer Authorization Formats (MAF), BIS safety certifications, CPWD compliance declarations, and customized BOQ technical schedules for central and state government tenders across India.

What electrical and structural preparations are required prior to installation?

Active LED walls require a flat, plumb masonry or reinforced drywall structure capable of supporting approximately 35 kg to 45 kg per sq meter. Electrically, a dedicated 3-phase 415V AC power supply with isolated copper earthing (neutral-to-earth voltage < 2V) and online UPS backup is required. GPSPL provides complete pre-installation CAD drawings, civil anchor locations, and electrical line single-line diagrams (SLDs) during Stage 01 of our project engagement.

What is the typical delivery and commissioning timeline?

Standard indoor displays (e.g. 110-inch to 165-inch all-in-one panels or standard P1.25/P1.5 walls) are typically delivered and commissioned within 7 to 14 business days from our Delhi NCR warehouse stock. Custom architectural curved newsrooms, multi-story facades, or high-security command centers generally require 3 to 4 weeks for precision CNC structure fabrication, civil integration, and multi-step optical spectrophotometer calibration.
