FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about design, implementation, brands, support, and Pan-India project execution.

Is Active LED Wall Installation different from Active LED Wall Solutions?

Yes. Active LED Wall Solutions covers product and solution planning, while Active LED Wall Installation focuses on site readiness, mounting, cabling, controller setup, calibration, commissioning and handover.

Can GPSPL install LED walls supplied by GPSPL?

Yes. GPSPL can supply LED display technology and also provide installation, commissioning, AMC and support.

What should be ready before LED wall installation?

Site measurements, power readiness, wall or structure details, cable routes, access clearance, content sources and controller location should be reviewed before installation.

Does GPSPL handle calibration?

Yes. GPSPL supports screen mapping, brightness checks, visual consistency checks, source testing and commissioning.

Can installation include controller and source switching?

Yes. GPSPL can coordinate controller setup, source routing, AV switching and control workflow depending on the project scope.

Does GPSPL provide training after installation?

Yes. GPSPL can provide operator guidance, handover documentation and basic troubleshooting orientation.

Is AMC available for installed LED walls?

Yes. GPSPL can provide preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, warranty coordination and post-installation support.
