WHAT KIND OF WORK YOU CAN EXPECT

GPSPL work sits at the intersection of cutting-edge product knowledge, site execution, customer coordination and long-term support.

Learning

Hands-on technology exposure

Teams work with professional AV, active LED walls, displays, projection, digital signal processors, AMX/Crestron automation, KVM routing, UPS power backup, IT infrastructure, and enterprise collaboration suites.

Ownership
