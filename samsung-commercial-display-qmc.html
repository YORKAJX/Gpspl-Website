Why Deploy the Samsung QMC Series?

Detailed breakdown of Samsung's flagship commercial signage platform designed for command centers, airports, corporate boardrooms, and high-traffic public concourses.

500 Nits & 25% Non-Glare Haze

High ambient brightness ensures vivid readability. The industrial 25% haze matte filter diffuses harsh artificial LED ceiling lights and direct window sunlight, completely eliminating mirror reflections.

24/7 Continuous Heavy-Duty Duty

Equipped with heavy-duty commercial grade capacitors, high-reliability LED backlights, and intelligent thermal management to operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without burn-in.

SmartView+ Wireless Collaboration

Enables simultaneous multi-screen wireless sharing for Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS devices. Ideal for executive meeting rooms and conference tables without requiring third-party HDMI dongles.

IP5x Certified Dust Protection

Certified IP5x rating protects internal electronics and circuit boards from microscopic dust accumulation in high-traffic train stations, airports, shopping malls, and production facilities.

Ultra-Slim 28.5mm Symmetrical Depth

Slim uniform thickness of just 28.5mm with a centered VESA mount. Guarantees clean flush wall mounting with symmetrical bezels allowing flawless landscape or portrait orientation.

Dual CMS: MagicINFO S10 & VXT Cloud

Support for enterprise MagicINFO S10 on-premise servers and modern Samsung VXT Cloud CMS. Remote device diagnostics, automated scheduling, firmware updates, and PlayLock PIN protection.

Technical Data Sheet

Samsung QMC Comprehensive Specifications

Display Panel
  • Screen Sizes Available: 43", 50", 55", 65", 75", 85"
  • Native Resolution: 4K UHD (3,840 × 2,160)
  • Brightness (Typ.): 500 nits (High Ambient Output)
  • Anti-Glare Haze Filter: 25% Non-Glare Matte Surface Treatment
  • Contrast Ratio: 4,000:1 (High Dynamic Contrast)
  • Viewing Angles: 178° Horizontal / 178° Vertical
  • Daily Operating Hours: 24 Hours / 7 Days Continuous Heavy-Duty
  • Orientation Support: Landscape & Portrait (0° / 90° pivot)
SOC & Platform
  • Operating System: Tizen 7.0 (Samsung Smart Signage Platform)
  • Processor (CPU): Cortex-A73 1.6GHz Quad-Core High-Speed Processor
  • On-Board Storage: 16GB Flash (10GB user available for offline content playback)
  • Content Management: MagicINFO S10, SSSP 10.0 & Samsung VXT Cloud CMS
  • Wireless Collaboration: SmartView+ multi-device screen mirroring
Video & Audio I/O
  • DisplayPort: 1x DP 1.2 Input with HDCP 2.2
  • HDMI Inputs: 3x HDMI 2.0 (HDCP 2.2 compliant)
  • USB Ports: 2x USB 2.0 Type-A
  • Audio Output: 1x Stereo 3.5mm Mini Jack Out
  • Internal Speakers: Built-in 2-Channel 10W + 10W stereo speakers
Network & Control
  • Ethernet LAN: 1x RJ45 Gigabit Ethernet port
  • Serial Control: RS-232C (In/Out) via Stereo Jack for Crestron/AMX/Extron IP Control
  • Wireless Networking: Dual-Band Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) & Bluetooth 5.2
  • IR Control: Built-in ambient sensor & External IR Receiver Input
Mechanical & Mounting
  • Chassis Depth: 28.5 mm Ultra-Slim Symmetrical Profile
  • Bezel Width: Narrow Symmetrical Bezel (11.5mm top/sides)
  • VESA Mount: 200×200 (43"), 400×300 (50"), 400×400 (55"/65"), 600×400 (75"/85")
  • Dust Protection Rating: IP5x Certified Enclosure
Warranty & Support
  • Standard Warranty: 3-Year Onsite OEM Commercial Warranty from Samsung India
  • GPSPL SLA Support: Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), preventive checkups & hot-standby unit replacement
