Sourcing Advice

Which brand or product family is right for your project?

Selecting the right manufacturer ensures compatibility, performance, and lifecycle uptime. GPSPL advises and sources technology based on seven key project criteria.

Application Suitability

We match hardware to your usage pattern—selecting 24/7 high-uptime panels for control rooms, and budget-friendly 16/7 displays for standard corporate signage.

Room Dimensions

Selecting display sizes (e.g. 65", 75", 86" interactive boards) and microphone pickup ranges based on seating layout and audience dimensions.

Ecosystem Compatibility

Ensuring new equipment communicates seamlessly with your existing video switcher matrices, control processors, and software platforms (Teams/Zoom).

Project Budget

Mapping your procurement requirements to the right brand tier (e.g., premium room automation vs. cost-effective manual matrix routing).

Warranty & Support

Selecting brands with localized service centers in Delhi NCR to ensure rapid shipping, part replacement, and hassle-free warranty validation.

Installation Conditions

Evaluating wall structures for heavy active LED arrays, planning ceiling layouts for microphones, and verifying cabling routes before procurement.

FAQ

Brand Sourcing & Support FAQs

Common queries from enterprise buyers and IT managers regarding brand procurement, installation, and lifecycle support.

Does GPSPL supply only products or also install them?

We do both. GPSPL connects technology distribution with professional system integration. We can source the hardware directly and provide complete site survey, structured cabling, rack dressing, device mounting, DSP tuning, programming, and commissioning support.

Can GPSPL help compare brands for our project?

Yes. Our engineering and solution consulting teams help compare different brands based on features, interface design, price points, physical dimensions, warranty periods, and compatibility with your existing environment.

Can GPSPL support warranty coordination?

Yes. For products sourced through GPSPL, we manage the entire manufacturer warranty process. In case of hardware failures, we diagnose the issue, coordinate device registration, unmount the unit, ship it to the authorized brand repair center, track shipping timelines, and remount the serviced hardware.

Does GPSPL provide AMC for partner products?

Yes. We offer multi-brand AV AMC contracts. Our AMC service covers regular preventive maintenance checks, display recalibration, firmware updates, and rapid on-site troubleshooting with dedicated standby hardware in Delhi NCR.

Can GPSPL prepare a BOQ with multiple brands?

Yes. We design hybrid, best-of-breed systems. We map displays from one brand (like LG or Samsung), audio capture from another (like Sennheiser or JBL), and control from AMX or Crestron, ensuring all components interface smoothly via standard switcher matrices.

Can GPSPL work with existing equipment?

Yes. During our initial site survey, our engineers audit your existing audio, display, cabling, and power equipment. We identify which components are functional and compatible, and integrate them cleanly into the upgraded system.
