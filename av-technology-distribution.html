Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial AV Distribution & B2B Wholesale FAQs

Which brands does GPSPL distribute in India?

GPSPL is an authorized Tier-1 Regional Technology Distributor for world-leading AV and enterprise hardware manufacturers, including Samsung Commercial Displays, LG Business Solutions, Harman Professional Audio (JBL Professional, Crown, Soundcraft, BSS, dbx), Crestron Electronics, Poly (an HP company), Barco (ClickShare), Kramer Electronics, Sennheiser, and Wacom.

How does Deal Registration work for AV System Integrators?

System integrators and contractors who discover and register an enterprise project opportunity with GPSPL receive project price protection, exclusive OEM manufacturer authorization letters (MAF), and special project-specific discounts that prevent other competitors from undercutting their margins.

Can GPSPL provide Manufacturer Authorization Format (MAF) for GeM and Government tenders?

Yes. As an authorized direct distributor, GPSPL coordinates directly with OEMs (Samsung, LG, Harman, Poly, etc.) to issue valid, verifiable Manufacturer Authorization Format (MAF) letters, tender compliance certificates, and warranty commitment undertakings for public sector and GeM portal bids.

What are your dispatch and delivery timelines across India?

We maintain ready warehouse inventory at our central distribution hub in Delhi NCR (Nehru Place / Greater Noida). In-stock items (commercial displays, soundbars, mics, wireless switchers) are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours with insured freight delivery to all major tier-1 and tier-2 cities across India.
