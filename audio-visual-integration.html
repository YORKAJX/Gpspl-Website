Expert Engineering Insights FAQ

Frequently Asked AV Integration Questions

Architectural answers regarding turnkey integration, Dante network audio, hardware echo cancellation, and enterprise maintenance SLAs.

Why choose a single turnkey AV integrator instead of multiple separate vendors?

Hiring multiple independent vendors for displays, acoustic panelling, microphones, and control programming inevitably leads to finger-pointing when audio echo occurs or video matrixing lags. GPSPL provides single-point accountability across the entire lifecycle: architectural engineering, Tier-1 OEM wholesale procurement, Dante network configuration, structured rack dressing, and a unified 4-hour onsite SLA.

How does GPSPL guarantee zero acoustic echo and feedback in modern glass-wall spaces?

Modern corporate offices with floor-to-ceiling glass and hard marble floors suffer from severe sound reflection (RT60 > 1.0s). GPSPL resolves this via hardware Acoustic Echo Cancellation (AEC) running on dedicated enterprise DSP cores (Q-SYS, Biamp, Crestron) tuned to converge voice signals in under 0.25 seconds while filtering HVAC rumble, paired with discreet architectural acoustic wall treatments.

What is the difference between traditional AV switching and AV-over-IP (Dante / SDVoE)?

Traditional fixed-chassis matrix switchers (e.g. 8x8 or 16x16) are bulky, expensive to expand, and require specialized point-to-point HDMI/HDBaseT cabling. AV-over-IP (such as Crestron NVX, SDVoE, and Dante audio) encodes uncompressed 4K60 video and studio audio directly onto standard enterprise Gigabit/10G network switches, enabling virtually unlimited inputs and outputs that can be scaled simply by adding network endpoints.

Can GPSPL prepare an architectural AV BOQ before conducting a physical site survey?

Yes. By analyzing your architectural floor plans, room dimensions, target seating capacity, and UC platform preferences (Teams, Zoom, Webex), our Avixa-certified design engineers can generate a preliminary planning BOQ with itemized hardware options and budget ranges within 24 hours. Exact cable conduit lengths and mounting brackets are finalized during our complimentary onsite survey.

What ongoing maintenance and emergency response SLAs does GPSPL offer?

Every turnkey deployment includes a 1-year comprehensive warranty backed directly by Tier-1 OEMs (Shure, Sennheiser, Crestron, Poly, Sony), plus GPSPL's Enterprise AMC SLA providing guaranteed 4-hour onsite response across Delhi NCR, bi-monthly proactive firmware audits, remote network telemetry health checks, and priority loaner equipment during unexpected hardware servicing.
