Projector & Accessories FAQs

What are the benefits of laser projectors over traditional lamp-based projectors?

Laser projectors offer longer lifespans (up to 20,000 hours vs. 3,000 hours for lamps), faster startup/shutdown times, more stable brightness over time, and require no periodic lamp replacement, resulting in a lower total cost of ownership (TCO).

How does throw ratio impact projector selection?

Throw ratio determines how far back a projector must be placed from the screen to project an image of a specific width. Short-throw and ultra-short-throw projectors can sit close to the wall, preventing presenter shadows, while long-throw models are ideal for large auditoriums.

What projector brands does GPSPL supply?

GPSPL is an authorized partner and integrator for Epson, Sony, Panasonic, and BenQ business projectors and accessories, coordinating direct factory warranty support.

Can we project wirelessly from our smartphones or laptops?

Yes. We integrate wireless presentation hardware (like Barco ClickShare or BenQ InstaShow) that allows presenters to share screen contents instantly with a single button click or application connection.

Comparison Guide

Laser Projector Setup vs. Active LED Display Wall

Key differences for large-venue displays and lecture hall environments.

Parameter Laser Projector & Screen Active LED Display Wall
Ambient Light Handling Prone to washout in brightly lit rooms; requires dimming lights. Vibrant display in direct sunlight/ambient light (600-1000+ nits brightness).
Installation Space Requires throw distance from ceiling mount to wall screen. Flat mount directly onto the wall structure, saving ceiling/floor space.
Shadow Obstruction Presenters can block the projection beam, casting shadows. Direct emission display, zero presenter shadow or glare issues.
Total Cost of Ownership Lower initial cost; periodic filter cleaning and alignment checks. Higher initial cost; extremely low maintenance and 100,000h module life.
