Choose the display around its location

Tell us whether the standee will sit inside a store, beside a bright window, in a mall corridor or outdoors. Screen size alone does not determine suitability. Confirm brightness, operating hours, orientation, stability, ventilation and service access against the selected model.

USB playback or centrally managed digital signage?

A locally updated screen may suit one location. A network of stores may need scheduled campaigns, user permissions and remote content updates. Confirm supported media formats, player requirements, network access and CMS licence costs before ordering.

Single-unit and bulk quotations

Single-unit requests are welcome. For multiple units, provide quantities by screen size and location, delivery schedule and installation requirements. Ask for hardware, player, stand or enclosure, shipping, CMS, installation and training to be itemised. Stock and lead times depend on the selected configuration.

Digital standee, interactive kiosk or Active LED?

A digital standee displays content; touch interaction requires suitable touch hardware and software. A larger seamless promotional surface may require an Active LED wall. Share the intended visitor experience so the proposal covers the right solution rather than only a screen size.
