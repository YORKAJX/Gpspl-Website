Frequently Asked Questions: Hotel & Hospitality AV

What audio visual solutions does GPSPL provide for hotels and resorts?

GPSPL provides end-to-end turnkey hospitality AV integration across India. Our scope includes high-impact Active LED video walls for grand ballrooms and banquet stages, high-SPL Harman JBL line-array sound reinforcement, Dante-enabled multi-zone background music (BGM) matrices for fine dining restaurants and lobbies, LG Pro:Centric and Samsung commercial hotel TVs for guest rooms, interactive wayfinding digital totems, and corporate meeting room video conferencing.

What equipment is included in a hotel banquet and grand ballroom AV setup?

A complete hotel banquet AV setup includes fine-pitch P1.86 to P2.5 indoor Active LED video walls capable of overpowering bright chandeliers, high-headroom line-array speakers, digital audio consoles, wireless handheld and lapel microphone systems (Sennheiser/Shure), motorized partition audio switching matrices, stage lighting coordination, and centralized Crestron/AMX iPad control.

How does a multi-zone background music (BGM) system work for hotel restaurants and lobbies?

GPSPL engineers multi-zone audio networks using digital signal processors (BSS Soundweb / Symetrix / Yamaha) with Dante/AES67 audio-over-IP. This allows hotel managers to control volume, select independent playlists, and make emergency paging announcements for individual zones (Lobby, All-Day Dining, Bar Lounge, Poolside, Spa) from wall-mounted keypads or mobile tablets.

Does GPSPL supply authorized LG Pro:Centric and Samsung Hospitality TVs?

Yes. GPSPL is an authorized Tier-1 commercial technology distributor and partner for LG Business Solutions and Samsung Commercial Displays. We supply specialized hospitality TVs (LG NU88C / UA831C series, Samsung BEFX series) featuring Hotel Mode, custom welcome channels, anti-theft PIN lock, centralized cloud CMS, and direct OEM warranty paperwork.
