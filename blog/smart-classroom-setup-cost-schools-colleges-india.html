2026 Smart Classroom Cost Benchmark

Under the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), Indian classrooms are upgrading from projector-whiteboard setups to 4K Interactive Flat Panels (IFPs) with digital whiteboarding. A turnkey smart classroom costs between ₹1.25 Lakhs (Tier 1 Basic) to ₹3.10 Lakhs (Tier 2 Standard) and ₹5.50 Lakhs+ (Tier 3 Advanced Hybrid/Lecture Capture).

2. Detailed Equipment Breakdown for Education AV

Samsung Flip, LG CreateBoard, ViewSonic, or Maxhub. 4K anti-glare toughened glass, zero bonding, 40-point multi-touch, wireless screen sharing.

Digital Smart Podium

Integrated touch monitor, gooseneck microphone, document camera scanner, amplifier, and central controller in a lockable metallic chassis.

Auto-Tracking PTZ Camera

Tracks the teacher automatically as they move across the board without requiring a dedicated camera operator. USB 3.0 & HDMI streaming.

Classroom Voice-Lift Audio

Ensures the teacher’s voice is evenly heard at the last bench without vocal strain. Includes UHF rechargeable wireless lapel mics.

3. Institutional Procurement FAQs

Why choose Interactive Flat Panels over projectors for schools?

Interactive Flat Panels eliminate projector bulb replacements (saving ₹8,000–₹15,000/year per room), cast zero shadow from the teacher's hand, work clearly in daylight without dimming room lights, and have a 50,000+ hour operational lifespan.

Does GPSPL offer teacher training after installation?

Yes. Every GPSPL educational installation includes full on-site faculty enablement sessions covering digital whiteboarding tools, subject teaching software, wireless mobile casting, and remote lesson recording.
