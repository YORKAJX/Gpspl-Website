AMC vs One-Time Repair: What's Better for AV Systems in Delhi NCR?

Should you sign an Annual Maintenance Contract or pay per repair? We break down the real cost difference, what AMC actually covers, and when each model makes financial sense for your organisation.

The Real Question

When an organisation installs a conference room AV system, a video wall, or an auditorium audio setup, the next decision is almost always: "How do we maintain it?"

Two models are available: an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) — a fixed annual fee for regular servicing and breakdown support — or on-demand / one-time repairs — paying per visit when something breaks. The right choice depends on your system complexity, usage intensity, and risk tolerance.

What Does an AMC Cover?

A well-structured AMC from GPSPL typically includes:

  • Preventive maintenance visits — quarterly or bi-annual inspection, cleaning, cable checks, firmware updates
  • Priority breakdown response — guaranteed response time (typically 4–8 business hours for critical systems)
  • Labour cost coverage — no per-visit charges for covered breakdowns
  • Consumable replacement — projector lamps, batteries, minor parts (varies by AMC tier)
  • Software and configuration support — UC platform updates, control system reprogramming
  • Annual system health report — condition assessment, proactive replacement recommendations
Important Not all AMCs are equal. An AMC that only covers labour — but not parts — can still result in significant unexpected costs. Always clarify what is and is not covered before signing.

When AMC Makes Sense

An AMC is almost always the right choice when:

  • The system is used daily (conference rooms, training halls, reception screens)
  • You have a CFO or board presentation scheduled — downtime is unacceptable
  • The installation includes complex integration (Crestron/AMX control systems, DSP audio)
  • Your team has no internal AV support capability
  • The system is under warranty — an AMC ensures the warranty is not voided by unauthorised service
  • You operate a hotel, hospital, educational institution where disruption affects hundreds of people

When Break-Fix (On-Demand) Makes Sense

Pay-per-repair works reasonably well when:

  • The system is simple — a single display with a cable box
  • Usage is infrequent (monthly meetings, occasional events)
  • You have in-house IT support capable of basic AV troubleshooting
  • The installation is less than 1 year old and under full manufacturer warranty

Our Recommendation

For any AV installation above ₹2 lakh in a business environment, we recommend signing an AMC from year one. The cost of a single major breakdown — a failed DSP, a burnt amplifier, a misconfigured control system — can easily exceed the annual AMC fee. Beyond cost, the peace of mind and guaranteed response time are worth significantly more to most organisations.

Start with a Standard AMC in the first year to understand your system's support requirements, then upgrade to Professional if you find the response time or preventive visit frequency insufficient.
