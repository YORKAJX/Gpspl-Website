Active LED Video Wall Guide: Pixel Pitch (P1.2 to P2.5), Sizing, Cost & Maintenance

How to calculate the right pixel pitch based on viewing distance, compare SMD vs COB LED technology, plan power & structural mounting, and evaluate real cost-per-sqft benchmarks in India.

1. Understanding Active LED Pixel Pitch

Active LED displays consist of modular LED cabinets seamlessly locked together. Pixel pitch (the center-to-center distance between adjacent LED pixels in millimeters) directly determines image sharpness and minimum viewing distance.

Formula for Optimal Viewing Distance

As a proven engineering rule of thumb:

Optimal Viewing Distance (in meters) ≈ Pixel Pitch (in mm) × 1.0 to 1.5
  • P1.25 mm: Ideal for executive boardrooms and command centers where viewers sit within 1.5 to 3 meters.
  • P1.53 / P1.86 mm: The industry standard for corporate lobbies, town halls, and auditorium backdrops (3 to 6 meters distance).
  • P2.5 mm: Cost-effective choice for large auditoriums, banquet halls, and retail atriums (6+ meters distance).

2. SMD vs COB vs GOB Technology

  • SMD (Surface Mounted Diode): Mature, highly cost-effective technology with great color fidelity, best for controlled indoor spaces.
  • GOB (Glue on Board): Epoxy resin coating over SMD diodes provides moisture, dust, and impact protection—great for high-traffic corporate reception areas.
  • COB (Chip on Board): Direct mounting of micro-LED chips on substrate delivers ultra-deep contrast, superior heat dissipation, and zero pixel drop-off risk.

3. Power, Cooling & Video Controller Planning

Active LED walls require dedicated 3-phase power distribution, synchronized NovaStar/Colorlight video processors with 4K HDMI/DisplayPort inputs, and structural load validation for wall reinforcement.
