Active LED Video Wall Guide: Pixel Pitch (P1.2 to P2.5), Sizing, Cost & Maintenance

How enterprise facility managers and IT architects calculate the exact pixel pitch, evaluate SMD vs COB technology, plan power and structural load, and benchmark real cost-per-square-foot investments in India.

1. Understanding Active LED Pixel Pitch & Viewing Distance

An Active LED display consists of modular die-cast aluminum cabinets containing LED pixel modules seamlessly locked together with zero visible bezels. Pixel Pitch is the physical center-to-center distance between adjacent LED diodes measured in millimeters (e.g., P1.25, P1.53, P1.86, P2.5).

Formula for Optimal Viewing Distance Minimum Viewing Distance (Meters) \(\approx\) Pixel Pitch (mm) \(\times\) 1.0 to 1.5
If a viewer sits closer than this distance, they will begin to see individual pixel dots rather than a smooth, continuous image.

2. SMD vs COB vs GOB LED Packaging Technologies

The manufacturing encapsulation method determines the durability, contrast ratio, and maintenance profile of your LED wall:

SMD (Surface Mount)

Traditional individually soldered LEDs. Most cost-effective, easily field-repairable at single module level.

Best for: P1.86 to P2.5 mm
COB (Chip on Board)

Micro-LEDs bonded directly to PCB with epoxy coating. Zero pixel loss from physical impact, deep blacks (10,000:1 contrast).

Best for: P0.9 to P1.5 mm Boardrooms
GOB (Glue on Board)

Protective transparent resin applied over SMD modules. Waterproof, dustproof, and impact-resistant for high-traffic zones.

Best for: Retail & Public Lobbies

3. NovaStar Video Processing & Signal Architecture

An Active LED display requires an intelligent hardware processor to scale incoming HDMI/DP signals and map them precisely across pixel coordinates:

  • NovaStar VX400 / VX600: All-in-one controller + video switcher supporting up to 3.9 million pixels with 4–6 Gigabit Ethernet output ports.
  • NovaStar NovaPro UHD Jr: 4K master controller with ultra-low latency, Genlock synchronization, and seamless picture-in-picture (PIP) for live broadcasting.
  • Taurus Series (TB30 / TB50): Cloud-managed asynchronous multimedia player for standalone digital signage and retail atriums with remote 4G/Wi-Fi CMS updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lifespan of an indoor Active LED wall?

Enterprise indoor LED panels are rated for 100,000 hours of continuous operation (equivalent to 11+ years at 24/7 duty cycle) before reaching 50% brightness.

How are damaged LED pixels serviced?

Modern cabinets feature front magnetic service access. A technician uses a magnetic vacuum tool to pull out the individual module in 10 seconds and replace it with a calibrated spare without turning off the entire wall.

Why choose Active LED over traditional LCD Video Walls with bezels?

Active LED has 0.0mm physical bezels (completely seamless), 2x higher brightness (600–1000 nits vs 400 nits), higher refresh rates (3840Hz), and does not show dark grid lines through presentation slides or human faces.
