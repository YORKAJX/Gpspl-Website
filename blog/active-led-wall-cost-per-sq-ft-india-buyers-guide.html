Active LED Wall Cost per Sq Ft in India (2026 Price & BOQ Guide)

Planning an active LED video wall for a corporate boardroom, reception lobby, auditorium, or command center? Here is the exact cost per square foot for P1.2 to P4 pixel pitch, component pricing, structural fabrication, and how to avoid costly procurement mistakes.

Quick Executive Summary (2026 Rates)

Active LED video walls in India cost between ₹4,200 to ₹22,000 per sq. ft. depending primarily on Pixel Pitch (distance between LED centers), Cabinet Type (Die-Cast vs Sheet Metal), and LED Packaging (SMD vs COB). For a standard 135-inch 16:9 full HD boardroom display (approx. 55 sq ft), total turnkey cost ranges from ₹4.5 Lakhs to ₹9 Lakhs including video processing, fabrication, and installation.

2. What Else Goes into an Active LED Turnkey BOQ?

A common mistake procurement managers make is only budgeting for the display panels. An enterprise-grade Active LED installation requires five integral components:

4K Video Splicing Controller

Novastar VX400 / VX1000 / NovaPro UHD. Handles input switching (HDMI, DP, SDI), PIP windowing, and EDID scaling.

Structural Fabrication

Precision laser-cut mild steel (MS) framework or front-maintenance hydraulic brackets. Critical for 0.1mm alignment.

Power Distribution Box (PDB)

Sequenced power start-up to avoid in-rush current trip, with MCB breakers, surge suppressors, and phase balancing.

Calibration & AMC Warranty

Chroma & luminance calibration with spare module bank and guaranteed 4-hour SLA on-site repair replacement.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an Active LED display wall last?

Commercial Active LED walls are rated for 100,000 operational hours (approx. 10+ years at 24/7 operation). With front-serviceable vacuum tools, any faulty LED module or IC chip can be hot-swapped in under 60 seconds without dismantling the wall.

Is Active LED better than LCD Video Walls?

Yes. Traditional LCD video walls have visible bezel grid lines (0.88mm to 3.5mm seams) that cut through spreadsheets and video faces. Active LED is 100% seamless, 2x brighter (600–1000 nits vs 450 nits), and has no risk of panel burn-in.
