Digital Podium & Smart Electronic Lectern Guide 2026: Setup, Features & BOQ

How premier universities, medical colleges, and government auditoriums eliminate stage clutter by integrating motorized interactive touch screens, gooseneck microphones, document visualizers, and central AV switching into a single smart lectern.

1. What is an All-in-One Smart Digital Podium?

Traditional wooden podiums require messy loose microphone wires, external laptop cables, and separate remotes that confuse visiting speakers. A Digital Smart Podium (Electronic Lectern) houses an entire presentation control hub within a metallic motorized enclosure.

Presenter Convenience at the Stage The presenter stands at the lectern, writes notes or draws diagrams on the 21.5" touch screen with zero latency, and the audience sees the synchronized slide with live ink annotations on the main 200" stage screen.

2. Core Hardware Stack Inside a Smart Podium

1. 21.5" Motorized Touch Display

Full HD IPS panel with electromagnetic stylus pen pressure, palm rejection, and electric angle tilt adjustment.

2. Professional Audio & Mics

Dual gooseneck condenser mics with LED talk rings, wireless collar mic receiver, and built-in 100W speaker amplifier.

3. Central AV Controller

Push-button matrix switcher to toggle between internal PC, guest laptop USB-C/HDMI, document camera, and stage screens.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are digital podiums GeM (Government e-Marketplace) tender compliant?

Yes! GPSPL is an authorized supplier on the Government GeM portal offering standardized digital smart podiums compliant with all central and state educational procurement guidelines.

Can a visiting faculty member connect their own MacBook or iPad?

Yes! The podium side panel features a pop-up cubby with USB-C (with 60W charging), HDMI, and USB touch passthrough, allowing any external laptop or tablet to be displayed on stage instantly.
