Hospital & Healthcare AV Integration: From Surgical OT Streaming to Patient Digital Signage

GPSPL Healthcare Solutions • September 2026 • 7 Min Read

Modern hospitals and academic medical institutions in India require zero-latency, high-precision visual and communication technologies. Whether it is live broadcasting a complex robotic laparoscopic surgery from the Operation Theater (OT) to 500 attending surgeons in an auditorium, or facilitating international multi-disciplinary oncology board meetings via telemedicine, audio-visual reliability is literally a matter of life and death.

1. Live Surgical OT 4K Camera Transmission & Video Over IP

Traditional analog video routing cannot handle the sub-millisecond latency and uncompressed color accuracy needed for surgical education. We engineer dedicated Software Defined Video Over Ethernet (SDVoE / AV-over-IP) backbones running over 10GbE fiber optic cables.

  • Zero Latency (< 0.1 ms): Surgeons and students in remote lecture halls see needle movements with absolute real-time fidelity.
  • Multi-View Picture-in-Picture: Displays endoscopic feeds, surgical light cameras, patient vitals, and fluoroscopy on a single screen.
  • Sterile Field Integration: Voice-activated intercoms and foot-pedal controllers allowing surgeons to talk to auditorium students without breaking aseptic technique.

2. Telemedicine & Medical Boardroom Collaboration

Teleconsultation suites require crystal-clear voice clarity so radiologists, pathologists, and surgeons can debate cases with global specialists over Microsoft Teams or Zoom Rooms.

We deploy Shure MXA920 ceiling beamforming microphones with advanced acoustic echo cancellation (AEC) and DICOM-calibrated clinical displays (from LG and Barco) ensuring X-rays and MRI scans are viewed with true grayscale accuracy.

3. Centralized Patient Queue & Emergency Digital Signage

High-volume OPD corridors require continuous 24/7 commercial displays (Samsung BEFX / LG UR640S) that show token queue callouts, doctor availability, and health awareness video reels.

  • Cloud CMS Synchronization: Hospital IT staff can update announcements, doctor room assignments, or emergency codes across 100+ screens simultaneously.
  • Fire & Emergency PA Override: In case of an alarm, displays and ceiling speakers instantly switch to emergency evacuation routes.
