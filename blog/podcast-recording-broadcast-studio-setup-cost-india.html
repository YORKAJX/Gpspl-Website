2. Acoustic Treatment & Soundproofing: The Non-Negotiable Core

The most common mistake when planning a video or podcast studio in India is spending 90% of the budget on expensive cameras while ignoring room acoustics. A ₹3 Lakh Sony FX3 camera in an untreated, echoey glass conference room sounds unprofessional, whereas broadcast microphones paired with a properly tuned room produce world-class fidelity.

Acoustic Architecture Essentials:

  • Wall Absorption: 50mm to 100mm fabric-wrapped 48–64 kg/m³ high-density Rockwool panels absorbing speech reflections between 250Hz and 4000Hz (NRC ≥ 0.85).
  • Bass Traps: Corner-loaded membrane or porous bass traps that absorb low-end chest resonances and HVAC rumble.
  • Sound Isolation (STC 45+): Double-stud drywall construction with acoustic sealant, drop-down automatic acoustic door seals, and double-glazed laminated glass viewing windows.
  • HVAC Silencing: Lined acoustic ducting with inline silencer baffles to drop ambient air-conditioning noise below NC-25.

4. Multi-Camera 4K Video Production & Switching

A standard professional podcast requires at least a 3-camera setup: Camera 1 (Host close-up), Camera 2 (Guest close-up), and Camera 3 (Wide shot of both participants).

  • Cinema Cameras vs PTZ: For cinematic bokeh (blurred backgrounds), full-frame cinema cameras like the Sony FX3 or Sony FX30 with 35mm/50mm f/1.8 prime lenses are superior. For automated, operator-free operation, 4K AI Auto-Tracking PTZ cameras (Lumens, Sony, PTZOptics) allow a single operator to switch angles via joystick.
  • Hardware Video Switcher: Blackmagic Design ATEM Mini Extreme ISO or ATEM Television Studio 4K allows real-time live switching, lower-third graphic overlays, and ISO recording of all camera feeds plus master mix directly to SSD.
  • Teleprompter System: 17-inch through-the-glass prompters mounted directly in front of the lens with HDMI feed from presenter laptop or iPad.
